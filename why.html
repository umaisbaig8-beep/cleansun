Why transparent solar

Why use only the roof?

Your building already has surfaces that receive sunlight every day.

Traditional vs transparent

Same idea, additional surfaces.

Both approaches generate electricity from sunlight. Transparent solar simply expands the available surface area to suitable architectural glass.

▦
Limited to the roof
Traditional solar
Roof→Solar panels→Electricity
🗂️
Adds suitable glass surfaces
Transparent solar
Roof + suitable glass surfaces→Solar generation
✨

Transparent solar is not positioned as a replacement for rooftop solar. It expands the conversation to additional, suitable architectural glass surfaces.
